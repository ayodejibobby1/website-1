Professional Digital Presence Systems

Your professional reputation should be easy to understand and easier to discover. We bring together the essential elements of your online identity to create a consistent, credible and well-structured digital presence. Every pillar works on two layers: the polished presence your audience sees, and the marketing engine underneath that captures and nurtures business opportunities.

Professional Website Design

Front-end

We design professional websites that communicate your expertise, experience, achievements and services. Your website becomes a central place where potential clients, employers and collaborators can learn about your work.

Back-end engine

Behind the design, each website doubles as a consultation landing page with lead capture built in, so interested visitors can enquire or book a conversation through a clear, structured path.

Digital Identity & Profile Optimisation

Front-end

We help align your professional information across relevant online platforms, creating a consistent identity that accurately represents who you are and what you do.

Back-end engine

We do not optimise profiles for appearance alone. Each one is designed as the entry point of a complete Client Acquisition Pipeline, a sales funnel that guides the right people from first impression to first conversation.

Google Search Visibility

Front-end

We make your professional information easier for search engines to understand and discover, so the right people can find you when they look for your expertise.

Back-end engine

This is how we bring targeted, relevant organic traffic, the prospective clients, partners and stakeholders who matter to your work, into your wider digital ecosystem.

Foundational SEO & Website Indexing

Front-end

We strengthen the technical and structural foundations of your online presence through SEO fundamentals, Google Search Console, website indexing support and relevant structured data.

Back-end engine

About AyodejiBobby Digital Solutions

AyodejiBobby Digital Solutions is a digital consultancy that helps executives, independent consultants and experienced professionals present their expertise properly online. A social profile alone rarely shows the full picture of your experience, achievements and services, so we bring it together through professional websites, digital identity optimisation and Google search visibility. We don't promise instant rankings or guaranteed search results. We build a solid digital foundation and a clearer, more consistent professional identity.

Ayodeji Bobby is the founder and Digital Presence Specialist behind the consultancy. His approach centres on clarity, practical solutions and credible digital foundations, shaped around each client's goals, audience and existing online presence.

Frequently asked questions

Who do you work with?

Executives, independent consultants and experienced professionals who want a stronger, more credible online identity.

Can you guarantee a Google ranking?

No. Nobody can honestly promise rankings. We build the technical and structural foundations that make your information easier for search engines to understand and discover.

Do I need a website if I already have a LinkedIn profile?

A social profile alone may not show the full picture of your experience, achievements and services. A website gives you one central place that you control.

What is Regional Search Visibility?

An optional service for professionals who want clients in specific locations. It covers location-based search opportunities, relevant service pages and local search foundations.

Do you offer ongoing support?

Yes. Digital Authority Management covers website maintenance, profile updates, search visibility monitoring and continuous improvements.

What are the back-end marketing systems?

Underneath each pillar we build the systems that capture and nurture opportunities: lead capture on your website, a client acquisition pipeline that begins with your profiles, and email marketing and follow-up systems that keep interested leads engaged through structured, professional communication. We do not promise rankings or guaranteed results.

How much does it cost?

It depends on your goals and your existing online presence. Send an enquiry and we will recommend a suitable solution.
