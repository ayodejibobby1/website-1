Privacy Policy

What information this website collects, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Who we are

This website belongs to AyodejiBobby Digital Solutions, run by Ayodeji Bobby, based in Abuja, Nigeria. You can reach us at ayodejibobbydigital@gmail.com.

What we collect

We only collect what you choose to send through the enquiry form on our homepage:

  • Your full name and email address
  • Whether you are an executive, consultant or professional, and your profession or industry
  • The services you are interested in
  • Your website or LinkedIn link and the location you want clients from, if you give them (optional)
  • When you would like to start, and the goals you describe in your message

If you message us by email or WhatsApp instead, we receive the details you include in that message.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and recommend a suitable solution
  • To prepare a quote and, if you go ahead, to deliver the work

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for anything unrelated to your enquiry.

Who processes it

  • Formspree receives form submissions on our behalf and forwards them to us by email. Their own privacy policy applies to how they handle that data.
  • Google Fonts supplies the website's fonts. Loading them sends your IP address to Google.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website and may record standard server logs, such as your IP address.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use advertising cookies or analytics tools. If we add them in future, we will update this page.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as we need them to respond to you and deliver our services, then delete them when they are no longer needed.

Your choices

You can ask us to tell you what information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, and you can ask us to stop contacting you. Email ayodejibobbydigital@gmail.com and we will respond.

Third-party links

Our pages link to LinkedIn, X, Instagram and WhatsApp. Those services have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
